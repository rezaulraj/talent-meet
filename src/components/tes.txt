1. Hero — the "wow" moment

This is where you spend your one big risk.

Canvas background: a slow-moving particle/node network (WebGL via three.js or plain <canvas>) where dots represent candidates and lines connect them to "placements" — subtly implies your actual business (matching people to jobs) instead of generic floating shapes.
GSAP: SplitText to reveal the headline word-by-word or character-by-character on load, staggered with a slight blur-to-focus effect. Pair with a Timeline so the nav, headline, subtext, and CTA button choreograph in sequence (nav first, then text, then button) rather than fading in together.
Micro-detail: an animated counter ticking up next to the headline ("12,000+ placed across 18 countries") using GSAP's ScrollTrigger + a number-tween plugin.
2. Trust bar (logos / accreditations)
Infinite horizontal marquee (GSAP ScrollTrigger isn't even needed here — a simple xPercent tween looping) of client logos or certification badges. Pause on hover.
3. Services overview
Cards on a staggered scroll reveal: ScrollTrigger with batch() so cards animate in as they enter viewport, staggered by ~0.1s, translateY + opacity.
On hover, a subtle magnetic cursor effect (card tilts toward cursor position using GSAP quickTo) — feels expensive without being loud.
4. Industries — the signature scroll moment

This is a great spot for a pinned horizontal scroll:

ScrollTrigger with pin: true and scrub: true turns vertical scroll into horizontal movement through industry cards (Construction → Healthcare → Manufacturing...) — like flipping through a deck. This is the kind of moment people screenshot.
Each card has a background canvas texture (subtle animated grain or gradient mesh unique to that industry) rather than a static photo.
5. How it works / process
An animated SVG path (a route line, echoing "sourcing across borders") that draws itself as you scroll, with numbered steps popping in at each vertex. ScrollTrigger scrub tied to stroke-dashoffset.
6. Global presence
Instead of a static map image: an SVG world map where country pins light up sequentially (staggered GSAP fromTo on opacity/scale) as the section enters view, with a soft pulsing glow (canvas radial gradient) on your active countries — makes "we operate globally" tangible.
7. Stats / impact
A dark, high-contrast band with big animated numbers (candidates placed, years active, retention rate) — GSAP counts them up on scroll-into-view, easing out so they don't feel mechanical.
8. Success stories / testimonials
A draggable/swipeable carousel (GSAP Draggable plugin) rather than auto-rotating dots — feels premium and gives the user control.
9. Featured jobs
A live-feeling ticker or auto-scrolling row of job cards, pauses on hover, "view all" fades in the CTA at the end of the row.
10. Final CTA
Bring back a toned-down version of the hero's canvas particle effect — bookending the page — with the Contact Us button front and center. This kind of visual echo (not identical, but a callback) is what makes a page feel designed rather than assembled section-by-section.
